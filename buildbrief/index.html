GOOD TO KNOW

Frequently asked questions

Does BuildBrief use AI itself?

No. It structures the details you provide into a reusable prompt. Your chosen coding assistant performs the AI work.

Does it work with Codex or Claude Code?

Yes. Choose your assistant to tailor a few instructions, then copy the output into that tool. It does not connect to your account.

Do I need to know how to code?

No. Explain what you see and what you expect. If you know the stack, add it; otherwise leave it blank.

Is the generated brief guaranteed to fix the issue?

No prompt can guarantee a correct implementation. Ask the coding assistant to verify its changes, inspect the diff, and test important flows.
